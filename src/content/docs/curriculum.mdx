The course runs from a first simulated message to a capstone network you
design, break and fix. Modules 03–04 follow Meshtastic and 05–06 follow
MeshCore; the rest cover both systems with the same assumptions so you can
compare them fairly.

<CurriculumOutline />

## Labs

- [Routing playground](../labs/routing-playground/): available as a narrow first preset. It covers a
  Meshtastic broadcast diamond and a MeshCore known-path failure with recovery. Topology presets, the
  Meshtastic DM fallback, MeshCore first-contact path learning and asymmetric links are still being built.
- RF and link-budget calculator, packet inspector and settings bench, and field planner: being built. Until
  then, Module 01 works its examples by hand.
