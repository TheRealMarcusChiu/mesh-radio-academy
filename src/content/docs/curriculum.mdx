The course runs from a first simulated message to a capstone network you
design, break and fix. Modules 03–04 follow Meshtastic and 05–06 follow
MeshCore; the rest cover both systems with the same assumptions so you can
compare them fairly.

## Labs

The [lab index](../labs/) lists all four hardware-free labs:

- [RF sandbox](../labs/rf-sandbox/): available. It computes LoRa packet airtime for SF7–12 at 125, 250 or
  500 kHz, and a link budget with an optional assumed obstruction, from inputs you choose.
- [Routing playground](../labs/routing-playground/): available. Step through Meshtastic managed flooding
  and MeshCore known-path forwarding on five small topologies, including the Meshtastic DM fallback, MeshCore
  first-contact path learning, asymmetric links and an airtime panel priced by the RF sandbox's calculator.
- [Packet inspector and mismatch bench](../labs/packet-inspector/): available. Read synthetic packets over the
  air, on the phone link and in the app, then change one setting at a time and see which stage stops.
- [Field planner and capstone workbench](../labs/field-planner/): available. Plan a 3–5 node network, estimate
  battery runtime from stated assumptions, keep a field log with delivery ratios and sample sizes, and build
  and export your capstone report in the browser.
