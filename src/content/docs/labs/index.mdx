Every lab runs in your browser with simulated, labeled data. None needs a radio, an account or a network
connection, and none stores anything outside your browser. Results are teaching models, not range predictions or
hardware benchmarks.

| Lab | What you do there | Used in | Status in this build |
|---|---|---|---|
| [RF sandbox](./rf-sandbox/) | Change spreading factor, bandwidth, coding rate and payload bytes and watch packet airtime; work a link budget with a dB example and an obstruction toggle. | Modules 01, 07, 12 | Available |
| [Routing playground](./routing-playground/) | Step through Meshtastic managed flooding and MeshCore known-path forwarding on five small topologies, with every decision explained and cited. | Modules 04, 06, 07, 12 | Available |
| [Packet inspector](./packet-inspector/) | Inspect synthetic packets field by field, switch between air, client and app views, and diagnose one settings mismatch at a time. | Modules 03, 06, 09, 12 | Available |
| [Field planner](./field-planner/) | Plan nodes, roles and power, estimate battery runtime with stated assumptions, keep a field test log with a blank CSV, and build the capstone report. | Modules 09, 10, 12 | Available |

## Known gaps

- The routing playground's topologies, links and the mobile node's move are learner-set teaching values, not
  coverage predictions.
- Routing airtime uses the RF sandbox's calculator, but the packet sizes it prices are labeled hypothetical
  learning sizes, not exact firmware frame lengths.
- The packet inspector's packets are synthetic and not decryptable. Byte offsets appear only where the course read
  the pinned firmware layout; the byte order of Meshtastic's 4-byte header fields is left unstated because the
  source does not state it.

## How the labs fit the course

1. **RF sandbox:** how long one packet stays on air and how much margin a link has (Module 01).
2. **Routing playground:** who forwards each packet, and what happens when a link breaks (Modules 04 and 06).
3. **Packet inspector:** what each byte means, and which mismatches block a link at which layer (Modules 03, 06, 09).
4. **Field planner:** turn all of that into a plan, a test log and a capstone report (Modules 09, 10, 12).
