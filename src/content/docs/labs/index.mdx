Every lab runs in your browser with simulated, labeled data. None needs a radio, an account or a network
connection, and none stores anything outside your browser. Results are teaching models, not range predictions or
hardware benchmarks.

:::note[Integration status]
This page lists all four labs. Each is built and reviewed in its own work stream and joins the site when it is
integrated. A lab marked **integration pending** is not part of this build yet, so its link won't open here.
Use the lesson's worked examples, which cover the same ideas on paper, until it arrives.
:::

| Lab | What you do there | Used in | Status in this build |
|---|---|---|---|
| [RF sandbox](./rf-sandbox/) | Change spreading factor, bandwidth, coding rate and payload bytes and watch packet airtime; work a link budget with a dB example and an obstruction toggle. | Modules 01, 07, 12 | **Integration pending** |

## Known gaps

- The routing playground here covers the four-node diamond: a Meshtastic broadcast and a MeshCore known-path
  failure with reset and recovery. More presets come with the routing lab's integration.
- Routing airtime is reported only once it can use the RF sandbox's calculator with stated packet bytes. Until
  then the playground counts transmissions and leaves airtime out, rather than guessing byte lengths.

## How the labs fit the course

1. **RF sandbox:** how long one packet stays on air and how much margin a link has (Module 01).
2. **Routing playground:** who forwards each packet, and what happens when a link breaks (Modules 04 and 06).
3. **Packet inspector:** what each byte means, and which mismatches block a link at which layer (Modules 03, 06, 09).
4. **Field planner:** turn all of that into a plan, a test log and a capstone report (Modules 09, 10, 12).
